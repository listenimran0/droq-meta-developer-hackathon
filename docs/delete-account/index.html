Delete your account

Last updated: 6 September 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your DROQ account and associated personal data. This page is the web request path required for Google Play.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the same address you used to sign up (or the Google account email linked to DROQ).
  2. Subject line: DROQ account deletion
  3. Include your DROQ username (if you remember it).

Send request to:

developerimrankhan9@gmail.com

Email deletion request

What we delete

  • Your account login (email / Google link)
  • Profile (username, display name, avatar)
  • Drops you created, discoveries, reactions, friend links, squad membership, and related notifications where feasible

Timeline

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of a verified request. Some backups may take longer to fully expire (up to 90 days).

What we may keep briefly

  • Records needed for fraud/abuse prevention or legal compliance
  • Anonymized analytics that cannot identify you
