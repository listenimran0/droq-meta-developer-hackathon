# Privacy Policy — DROQ

**Last updated:** 6 September 2026

DROQ (“we”, “the app”) is a location-based discovery app. By using DROQ you agree to this policy.

## What we collect

- **Account:** email, username, display name, optional profile photo; if you use Google sign-in, name and photo from Google.
- **Location:** precise location while you use Discover / Leave / nearby alerts (and background location only if you enable nearby alerts). Location is required for the core experience.
- **Content you create:** Drop text, photos, voice notes, and related metadata (place, unlock radius, visibility).
- **Device / push:** push notification token if you allow notifications.
- **Usage (minimal):** basic app events to keep the service working (for example session start).

## How we use data

- To show nearby Drops, unlock content when you are close, and notify you when something is waiting.
- To run accounts, friends, squad hunts, reactions, and moderation/reporting.
- To show ads (via Google AdMob) if ads are enabled in your build.

We do **not** sell your personal data. We do **not** show exact Drop coordinates to other users in the normal discovery UI.

## Sharing

- Data is stored on **Supabase** (auth, database, storage).
- **Google** for optional Google sign-in and AdMob ads.
- We may share limited data if required by law or to handle abuse reports.

## Retention & deletion

- You can request account deletion by contacting us (see below). We remove or anonymize personal data when no longer needed, except where law requires retention.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke location / notification / camera / mic permissions in system settings (some features will stop working).
- Turn off nearby alerts in DROQ Settings.
- Use email or Google to sign in.

## Children

DROQ is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the minimum age in your country.
