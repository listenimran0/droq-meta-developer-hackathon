Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

DROQ is a free mobile application (package com.droq.app). Downloading and using the core app does not require a payment to us.

Free download

There is no purchase price for installing DROQ from Google Play (or any other store where we publish it). Because there is no charge for the download itself, there is nothing for us to refund for installing or using the free app.

Advertising

DROQ may show ads through Google AdMob. Ads are not a paid product you buy from us. Viewing or interacting with ads does not create a refundable transaction with DROQ.

In-app purchases & subscriptions

If DROQ later offers paid features, tips, or subscriptions through Google Play or the Apple App Store, those charges are processed by the store — not billed directly by DROQ.

  • Request refunds through the store where you paid (Google Play / App Store), using their refund tools and timelines.
  • We do not receive your full payment card details; the store is the merchant of record for those purchases.
  • If a purchase failed, duplicated, or was unauthorized, contact the store first, then email us with the order ID if you need help.

Changes
