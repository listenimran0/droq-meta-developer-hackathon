Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

DROQ (“we”, “the app”) is a location-based discovery app operated for the package com.droq.app. By using DROQ you agree to this policy. Website: https://droq.app.

What we collect

  • Account: email, username, display name, optional profile photo; if you use Google sign-in, name and photo from Google.
  • Location: precise location while you use Discover / Leave / nearby alerts (and background location only if you enable nearby alerts). Location is required for the core experience.
  • Content you create: Drop text, photos, voice notes, video (if enabled), and related metadata (place, unlock radius, visibility).
  • Device / push: push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Usage (minimal): basic app events to keep the service working (for example session start).
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and interaction data to show ads.

How we use data

  • To show nearby Drops, unlock content when you are close, and notify you when something is waiting.
  • To run accounts, friends, squad hunts, reactions, trails, and moderation/reporting.
  • To show ads (via Google AdMob) when ads are enabled in your build.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show exact Drop coordinates to other users in the normal discovery UI before unlock.

Sharing

  • Data is stored on Supabase (auth, database, storage).
  • Google for optional Google sign-in and AdMob ads.
  • We may share limited data if required by law or to handle abuse reports.

Retention & deletion

You can request account deletion via droq.app/delete-account or from inside the app (Settings). We remove or anonymize personal data when no longer needed, except where law requires retention. We aim to complete verified deletion within 30 days; backups may take up to 90 days to fully expire.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location / notification / camera / mic permissions in system settings (some features will stop working).
  • Turn off nearby alerts in DROQ Settings.
  • Use email or Google to sign in.
  • Reset advertising ID in your device settings to limit ad personalization where available.

Children

DROQ is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under the minimum age in your country.
