How it works

Three steps. Real places.

DROQ turns the streets around you into a quiet treasure map — content stays sealed until you’re close enough.

  1. 01

    Leave

    Plant a note, photo, voice, or puzzle at your exact spot.

  2. 02

    Discover

    Sense what’s waiting nearby. Distance, not coordinates.

  3. 03

    Unlock

    Walk close enough — the Drop opens where it was left.
